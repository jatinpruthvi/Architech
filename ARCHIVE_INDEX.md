## Current source of truth

Use `architecture/normative/final-three-phase-architecture.md` for implementation. It retains the complete approved capability set and organizes delivery into three phases:

1. **Phase 1:** Google-indexable foundation and first complete product.
2. **Phase 2:** SEO scale, advanced discovery, localization publication, and operational expansion.
3. **Phase 3:** Authority optimization, regional expansion, experimentation, commercial growth, and resilience.

The latest Google-first off-page authority recommendation is saved at `seo/authority/off-page-authority-google-first-appendix.md`.
