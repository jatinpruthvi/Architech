## The 5 moves that actually work for a new real estate site

### 1. Win the layer housing.com ignores: hyperlocal + long-tail keywords
Big portals optimize for "city + property type." They cannot hand-optimize thousands of micro-neighborhood pages well. You can.
- Target **"2 bhk for rent in [building name / micro-locality]"** — e.g., "2 bhk for rent in Lodha Belmondo," "3 bhk sale Powai near Hiranandani."
- Build a programmatic-but-unique page for every building/society you serve, with original details (floor plans, nearby schools, commute times, RERA status). Housing.com has thin templated pages here.
- Verify volume with Google Keyword Planner / Ahrefs free tools; even 10–50 searches/month × hundreds of pages = real traffic.

### 2. Dominate Google Business Profile (GBP) — this is your fastest win
This is where national portals are weakest. Google weighs local pack results heavily for "[service] near me" and city searches.
- Claim and fully complete your GBP: categories, hours, service areas, photos (uploaded weekly), products/services, Q&A.
- Get reviews with **keyword-rich text** (not just "great service" — ask happy clients to mention "bought a 2BHK in Whitefield through [YourBrand]").
- Post weekly GBP updates (new listings, market notes). Google treats GBP activity as a freshness signal.

### 3. Build E-E-A-T and trust signals — Google's real estate YMYL filter is brutal
Real estate money queries are "Your Money or Your Life." Google applies extra trust scrutiny.
- **About us / team pages** with real photos, RERA registration numbers, credentials, years of experience — and schema markup.
- Display RERA/agent license numbers prominently site-wide.
- Add a clear, real **Contact + physical address + phone**, matched to your GBP.
- Get cited/linked from local press, chambers of commerce, RERA directories, and association sites. These are the links that move YMYL rankings.

### 4. Technical SEO from day one (cheaper to build right than fix later)
- **Core Web Vitals**: ship fast — lean theme, lazy-load images, next-gen image formats, minimal JS. Mobile-first indexing is default now.
- **Schema**: `RealEstateListing`, `LocalBusiness`, `Organization`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`. Use `aggregateRating` only if you genuinely have reviews on your own site.
- Clean URL structure: `/property/2bhk-rent-whitefield-lodha-belmondo` — keywords in URL, readable.
- XML sitemap + robots.txt, canonical tags on every page, 301 strategy for any duplicate listing pages.
- Render listings server-side or pre-rendered (not client-side JS only) so Google can read them.

### 5. Content that big portals won't make (your differentiator)
- **Neighborhood guides** with original research: price trends by society, school catchment maps, actual commute times, crime data, future infrastructure projects (metro lines, flyovers).
- **Buyer/seller journey content**: "stamp duty calculator [city]," "how to check RERA status," "home loan eligibility [city]." These capture high-intent long-tail and build topical authority.
- **Original data**: local price indices, rental yield tables. Data = links = authority. This is the realistic path to eventually challenging housing.com on broader terms.

## What NOT to bother with early
- Don't chase "flats in [city]" — you'll burn months for page-3 rankings. Come back to these once you have 100+ quality backlinks.
- Don't buy links — Google's spam updates in 2024–2025 have been aggressive, and real estate is a flagged sector.
- Don't copy listing descriptions from portals — duplicate content kills you.
