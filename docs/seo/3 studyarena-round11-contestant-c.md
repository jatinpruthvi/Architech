# StudyArena

**Question:** I am creating new website for real estate, I want seo for only google. as I am creating from scratch what are the best SEO hack should I use so I can also beat housing.com and other old websites.

**Answered by** Contestant C · Round 11 · 27 Aug 2026

---

To beat massive established aggregators like Housing.com with a brand-new domain, you cannot compete head-to-head on generic keywords like "flats in Mumbai" or "buy house in Bangalore." They have years of domain authority that you cannot outrank overnight. 

Instead, you need a "David vs. Goliath" strategy that exploits their weaknesses: generic content, outdated crowdsourced listings, and slow page speeds. Here are the most effective SEO strategies for a new real estate site on Google.

### 1. Target Hyper-Local, Long-Tail Keywords
Big portals rely on broad categories. You need to capture high-intent buyers searching for extremely specific things.
*   **Don't target:** "2 BHK apartments in Pune"
*   **Do target:** "2 BHK apartments near Magarpatta IT park with EV charging" or "Vaastu compliant 3 BHK flats in Baner under 1.5 Cr"
*   **Action:** Find micro-neighborhoods, specific apartment complexes, and landmark-adjacent keywords using Google Autosuggest and related searches. Create dedicated landing pages for these exact queries.

### 2. Implement Programmatic SEO
Aggregators win by having thousands of pages. You can replicate this efficiently without writing every page by hand.
*   **Action:** Build a database of local variables (Property Type, Neighborhood, Landmark, Amenity).
*   **Execution:** Create a high-quality landing page template and generate URLs dynamically, such as `/villas-near-dps-school-gurgaon` or `/pet-friendly-apartments-indiranagar`. Ensure the data injected into each template is unique and valuable (distance to landmark, local transit points) to avoid Google's duplicate content penalty.

### 3. Use Deep Structured Data (Schema Markup)
You want Google to read your site as a structured real estate database, not just text. Housing.com does this, but many smaller sites fail at it.
*   **Action:** Add JSON-LD schema markup to every property page.
*   **Crucial Schemas:** 
    *   `RealEstateListing` (tells Google the price, status, and property type)
    *   `SingleFamilyResidence` or `Apartment`
    *   `Offer` (price and currency)
    *   `Place` / `PostalAddress` (exact geo-coordinates)

### 4. Create "Hyper-Niche" Neighborhood Guides
Housing.com typically relies on basic user-generated descriptions for neighborhoods. Beat them with genuine local expertise (E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust).
*   **Action:** Write comprehensive guides for specific micro-markets. Include:
    *   Original street-level photography (not stock photos).
    *   Interviews with current residents.
    *   Distance to major tech parks, top-rated schools, and upcoming metro stations.
    *   Historical price trends for that specific street or building.
    *   Embed YouTube walking tours of the neighborhood. 

### 5. Crush Them on Core Web Vitals (Site Speed)
Real estate aggregators are notoriously slow because they are bogged down with third-party ads, tracking scripts, and unoptimized user-uploaded photos. Google directly rewards faster sites.
*   **Action:** 
    *   Serve all property images in next-gen formats like **WebP** or **AVIF**.
    *   Implement aggressive **lazy-loading** (only load images when the user scrolls to them).
    *   Keep your DOM size small and minimize Javascript. Aim for a Google PageSpeed Insights score of 90+ on mobile.

### 6. Clean and Updated Inventory Only
A major frustration with old platforms like Housing.com is "ghost listings"—properties that were sold months ago but are left up to generate leads. 
*   **Action:** Make "100% Verified & Available" your USP. Google tracks user signals (pogo-sticking). If users click your site, find exactly what they want, and don't immediately bounce back to Google, your rankings will surge. Use a "Last verified on [Date]" badge prominently on your listings.

### 7. Dominate the Google Map Pack
For "realtor near me" or "real estate agents in [local area]" searches, Google prioritizes the Map Pack over organic blue links.
*   **Action:** Claim and optimize your Google Business Profile (GBP). Seed it with weekly updates, upload high-quality photos of properties and your office, and aggressively collect 5-star reviews from past clients. Include your target keywords naturally in the review responses.
