# Google-First Off-Page Authority Appendix

## Decision

The architecture needed an explicit off-page authority and digital PR workstream. The recommended approach is to start a compliant seed effort in Phase 1, scale relationships and original research in Phase 2, and compound industry authority in Phase 3.

Google confirms that link analysis and PageRank remain part of its ranking systems, and links help Google understand relevance and discover pages. However, the plan must use legitimate link earning and digital PR rather than a high-volume backlink campaign. Google prohibits buying ranking-passing links, excessive exchanges, automated link creation, paid guest posts with optimized followed links, low-quality directories, and manipulative partner pages.

## Phase 1 — Seed legitimate authority

Establish a baseline of referring domains and mentions; define target cities and topics; create three to six high-quality research or data assets; publish methodology and source pages; establish publisher, researcher, developer, broker, association, and community relationships; conduct expert interviews and original local research; pitch genuinely newsworthy findings; secure early earned mentions where editorially justified; and monitor brand mentions, links, anchor text, and qualified referral traffic.

Suitable assets include RERA explainers and methodology, city/locality market snapshots, locality comparisons, project-verification methodology, an India property glossary, public research tables, and local housing reports. Every asset needs an author, methodology, source links, date, limitations, update policy, and contact.

Government and RERA sites must be treated as sources, not implied endorsers. Developer and broker partnerships may include legitimate profiles, references, interviews, case studies, and data contributions, but contracts must not require followed links. Paid placements must be clearly disclosed and qualified with `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`.

## Phase 2 — Scale authority and relationships

Publish quarterly city/locality reports, recurring RERA and market data releases, expert commentary, developer/broker case studies, local media collaborations, housing and urban-planning research, original surveys where methodologically sound, conference/webinar/podcast participation, and genuinely relevant resource-page outreach. Measure which assets earn natural links and improve them based on real demand.

## Phase 3 — Build defensible authority

Create annual flagship India real-estate reports, proprietary locality intelligence, research partnerships, press/data collaborations, expert panels, regional-language research assets, developer and broker ecosystem programs, unlinked-mention campaigns, and digital PR experiments measured by qualified traffic and brand search.

## Measurement

Track relevant referring domains, editorially earned links, linked-asset performance, linked and unlinked brand mentions, qualified referral traffic, assisted leads and saved searches, brand-query growth, anchor-text distribution, campaign link velocity, sponsored/UGC/nofollow compliance, toxic or hacked-link alerts, and page-cluster authority.

Do not use backlink quotas such as “obtain 100 links.” Do not use automated Google rank scraping outside permitted terms. Use Search Console, permitted APIs, approved rank tools, referral analytics, and manual checks within applicable terms.

## Final principle

> Phase 1 makes the site technically indexable and begins earning legitimate recognition. Phase 2 scales original content, relationships, and qualified authority. Phase 3 compounds brand, research, and industry-level reputation.
